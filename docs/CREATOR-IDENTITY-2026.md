## Publishing boundaries

- The website’s Patreon link points to the public creator page. Podcast directory links and playable episodes have separate approval lists.
- Store public audio, captioned excerpts and approved artwork in the public podcast media directory only after approval. Keep original recordings, private feeds, guest releases and full member videos outside the site.
- Podcast title, description, artwork, visitor flows and rollout checks are maintained with the existing [podcast launch checklist](PODCAST-PLATFORM-LAUNCH.md).
- Every episode needs a real approved recording, a reviewed transcript or show notes, audience verification at the host, and a reviewed publication record. The optional chapter list stores increasing offsets from zero in whole seconds.
- Courses display their development status. Publish an enrollment path only after the lessons, access, rights and terms have been reviewed.
- The draft PR and protected Laboratory release workflow are the review and production gates. The website identifies its deployed revision at `/_health`.
