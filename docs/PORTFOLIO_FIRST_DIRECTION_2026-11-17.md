## Identity / goals
The site is **Devon Alan Akins's personal portfolio**, not a fictional research institute, an AI compute company landing page, a generic product showcase, or a cumulative AI usage scoreboard.

Primary public identity: independent software developer and research engineer. Real current engineering, professional operations, creative writing, and developing scientific computing interests coexist. Scientific computing / AI infrastructure is the technical specialization Devon is building toward, not proof of a deployed Lambda-scale platform.

The interface must communicate within seconds: who Devon is; what he builds; his current areas of work; how to inspect real sources; how to contact him. It should feel immersive and technically serious without presenting stage props as live evidence or hiding the work behind artistic flourishes.

## New information architecture
Desktop navigation and mobile tab shell use six inherited logical keys to retain existing keyboard/hash behavior:
- Home: personal introduction; primary selected projects; four observable source-qualified signals.
- Research (experience key): active mathematical instrument and clearly labeled future programs.
- Work (work key): practical project and implementation profiles, evidence links, honest status.
- Activity (activity key): full public GitHub feed/filter, approved Threads, source-provenance guidance.
- Writing (writing key): technical notes, original authored work and independent research writing.
- Contact: public email and verified approved social channels.

**First viewport:** mission statement, selected work and some live signals should be present on desktop; small-screen experience may scroll within the existing single-site shell rather than hide content. Preserve one coherent experience on iPhone Safari and desktop.

## Visual system
Dark mineral and graphite surfaces, warmer copper details, soft teal signal, architectural light shafts, thin precision rules, typography balanced between meaningful serif editorial headings and readable sans/mono instrument labels. Use a restrained original laboratory chamber backdrop as visual context **behind** content. Gradually reveal layers rather than introducing pseudo-technical HUD clutter or meaningless numbers. The content is professional, navigable and accessible before visual effects.

## Source contracts

- Work presence: verified owner-controlled reporter opt-in and staleness semantics; never equate music or public GitHub pushes with editor connectivity.
- Spotify: owner consent, music not a developer activity proof.
